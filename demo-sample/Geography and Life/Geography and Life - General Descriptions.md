
**Eldenreach**
A massive, circular forest surrounds central Veloria, separating Arcallis, Veylan, and small villages from the outer edges of the continent. Magical beings and bloodlines survive deep within its hidden glades. Border villages live in constant fear of Church raids due to their proximity to arcane resources. The Church and central nations have carved paths to ease access to the continent's borders and extend their control, causing the destruction of precious forests and much magical life in the process. Most of the forest is still unexplored, rumored to shelter sorcerous beasts, wildblood families, and spirits that reflect magic’s true nature.

**The Spine Mountains**
A jagged range cutting from western cliffs to the northern cold. Rich mineral veins draw miners and excavators from multiple nations and the Church, while older civilizations persist within the tallest mountains, hiding metals and relics away from the rest of Veloria. Somewhere here lies the rumored Ashen Spire—magic’s last sanctuary—though all expeditions by enemies to locate it have failed.

**General Life**
With magic in controlled cities already being rare and expensive and uncontrolled magic being only available in settlements farthest from the Church’s central influence, it goes without saying that the villages bound to controlled nations suffer the most from lack of healing and enchantment. Common workers and soldiers die from diseases untreated, leading to a rise in local disdain for Church policies, but not enough power to ever confront it. Travel between central areas is expedient thanks to developed roads, but checkpoints and tolls have increased. 

**Beyond Veloria**
Foreign continents and cultures exist just across the seas—some hostile, others curious—but few outsiders understand Veloria’s fear of magic or the grip of the Church. Many adventurers come to Veloria in search of work, glory, or deeper motives, and find Veloria both profitable and perilous - especially if they end up on the wrong side of the Church. Most learn quickly to hide anything that glows.
