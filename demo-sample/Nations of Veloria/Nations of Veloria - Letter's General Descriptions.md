**The Kingdom of Arcallis**

The largest and strongest nation. Arcallis is deeply tied with the Church and serves as the primary military enforcement of Purity across Veloria, commanding constant patrols and inspections to maintain its influence and secure the Church’s ideals. A strong monarchy promotes the face of order and covers the Kingdom’s potential wrongdoings, securing the centralization of the continent's power and popularity under the Church. 

The capital city of Dawncrest is the political heart of Church influence, rising in white stone and gold spires with lamps fueled by sacred oils. Every district is watched by Church wardens, and even the air feels judged. Other smaller Arcallan cities are orderly but bleak - military drills and recruitment in every square, sermon bells replacing joy. 

**The Republic of Veylan** - [[The Republic of Veylan - General Description]]
A wealthy center of trade and innovation. Publicly secular but discreetly reliant on Church favor. The rich upper class exchange favors within the Church, while the average citizen lives barely above poverty and must fend for themselves through any means. Lacking a formal military, many Veylani citizens enlist with Arcallis for survival and opportunity. In the capital city of Veyloris, canals and trade houses define the bustling center of commerce. Magic here is a luxury of the elite, locked behind noble gates or auctioned in private under the protection of brides and allegiances.

**The Freeholds of Kessra**
The diverse collection of independent clans located in the Eastern highlands. Clan loyalty supersedes religion. Magic is still feared but sometimes sheltered if it benefits the clan. The clans see mostly eye to eye and are officially neutral in Church matters, but tension rises as Purity Laws creep closer to uncovering their use of magic and intervening with their ways of living. The clans meet in Stonehall, a massive fort of connected longhouses surrounding a sky-piercing monolith, said to contain ancient power. The clans have become more united in order to protect their independence.

**The Duskport Confederacy**
Industrial port cities along the southern coast. A place of masked intentions: trade booms, smuggling thrives, and foreign influence seeps into streets crowded with innovations of factories and steam. Magic survives here, far from Church control and supported by accepting foreigners, but is more often used for personal gain than true good. The largest city of Duskport is a maze of industrial docks, dense housing, and foggy streets. Foreign ships offload inventions, people, and philosophies the Church would sometimes rather see burned.

**The Old North** 
A harsh, frozen frontier resisting Church domination. Ancient magical traditions live on in secrecy within the northern clans. Militarily hardened, rumored to be the only nation that could withstand a full Church crusade due to their environmental advantage and sheer numbers. Secret paths through the constant blizzards hide an unknown world from the vision of the church.
