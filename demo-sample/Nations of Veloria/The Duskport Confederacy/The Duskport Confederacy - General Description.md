
The Duskport Confederacy is a loose alliance of coastal city-states bound not by loyalty, but by fear of irrelevance. Positioned along Veloria’s southern shore, it is the beating heart of maritime trade — a place where foreign gold flows thicker than clean water, and every dock sells secrets more readily than fish. Fog rolls in from the sea, giving cover to smugglers, cutthroats, and innovators alike. The Church of the Dawn maintains minimal presence here given how far out of reach they are. Magic is by no means celebrated, but in Duskport, if it puts food on tables, keeps the factories running, and makes the rich merchants an extra buck, no Porter will look too closely. Within these maze-like streets, power is measured in crew membership, favors, and how many people would bleed before letting you drown.

The city of Duskport is the Confederacy’s crown jewel — a sprawling harbor city built on pilings and dark ambition. Oil-slick canals cut between stacked tenements, while towering factories cough steam into the ever-present fog. Barges from foreign lands crowd the ports, unloading contraband, ideas, and trouble under the watchful eyes of greedy street crews looking to control another piece of property. Church banners hang over the central plaza, but their sermons fade against the clatter of forges and the quiet deals made in shadowed taverns. Here, justice is purchased, and the only real law is that the rich don’t fall — and the poor don’t swim too close to the edge. Duskport is where fortunes are minted, reputations are shattered, and the Confederacy whispers its future into being.
