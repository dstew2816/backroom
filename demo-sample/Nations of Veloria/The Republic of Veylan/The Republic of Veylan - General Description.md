Veylan is the continent’s glittering lie — a republic in name, an oligarchy in truth.  
It prides itself on being the beating heart of trade, invention, and art — a land where intellect triumphs over faith. But behind that façade, every invention is patented through bribery, every policy dictated by profit, and every innovation financed by the same noble families that whisper prayers to the Church when it suits them.

It’s a place where **genius is currency**, and poverty is seen as a moral failing.  
For those born in its shadows, like Dönar, survival is the only ideology left.
