An artsy city of canals and glass towers.  
Bridges arch over waterways glowing with lanterns fueled by imported oil. Gilded gondolas carry merchants and envoys through mirrored corridors of water, where the lights never seem to go out — except in the slums.

The air smells of perfume, hot iron, and stagnant water.  
Elegant spires cast their reflections over shanties built from discarded cargo crates.

By day, the city glitters like a promise.  
By night, its reflections tremble — because no light here is truly pure.

### **Layout & Districts**

1. **The Ascendant Ring** — the innermost circle around the Veylani Assembly. Bureaucrats, magnates, and Church envoys dwell here behind marble façades and glass gardens.
    
    - You can’t enter without a sponsor’s crest or a signed permit.
        
    - The canals are patrolled by private guards
        
2. **The Artisan Quarter** — workshops, artificers, and innovators crowd the mid-level bridges.
    
    - The “Innovation Guild” licenses inventions and quietly censors anything that challenges Church-sanctioned science.
        
    - Failed experiments and broken constructs are dumped into the lower waterways.
        
3. **The Gilded Market** — a floating bazaar that moves weekly, hosted on chained barges.
    
    - Here, alchemical components are sold under noble patrons’ protection.
        
    - Exotic goods from Duskport, Kessra, and foreign lands flow through auctions every dusk.
        
4. **The Floodways** — the slum labyrinth under the main city, where the poorest live in damp tenements or drift-houses tied to bridge pylons.
    
    - Law exists only where coin demands it.
        
    - Illegal prize fights, drug dens, and smuggling tunnels thrive here.
        
    - The Church pretends this district doesn’t exist — until it needs scapegoats.

### **Atmosphere**

- The canals never freeze, thanks to a contract with the Church to receive the Dawn's blessing of warmth.
    
- Constant sunny days turn the streets reflective and disguise the city’s rot.
    
    
- People are warm and frequent the market areas, but only the higher classes live in the central city. Most middle class civilians live in the outer rings or nearby small towns.

**The Veylani Mindset:** “If you can afford morality, you’ve earned it.”

- **The Elite** — polished, eloquent, and utterly transactional. They host masquerades where alliances are made in coded compliments. Many have secondary estates in Arcallis or pay for Church “blessings” that protect their patents and lineages.
    
- **The Working Class** — artisans, scribes, couriers, and factory hands. They romanticize innovation, dreaming that one invention could lift them to fame. But the system ensures their ideas are bought cheap and rebranded by their patrons.
    
- **The Forgotten** — Children of debtors, gamblers, and addicts. They work the Floodways, run packages through dangerous channels, and sell their fists, bodies, or magic to survive.
    

Even the poor speak with the cadence of merchants — every word is a bargain.
